# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze restaurant sales, customer behavior, restaurant performance, and delivery operations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics and helps identify trends across sales, customers, restaurants, cuisines, and delivery performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales & Orders Overview

This page provides an overview of overall business performance.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Revenue: 165M
- Total Orders: 200K
- Average Order Value: 824.57
- Average Delivery Time: 44.55

**Visuals:**
- Orders Trend
- Revenue Trend
- Revenue by City
- Top 10 Restaurants
- Order Status Breakdown
- City Filter

---

### 2. Customer Analysis

This page focuses on customer behavior and customer segmentation.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Customers: 20K
- Repeat Customers: 10K
- Repeat Customer %: 50.28%
- Orders per Customer: 8

**Visuals:**
- Signup Trend
- Orders by Customer Type
- New vs Repeat Customers
- Top 10 Valuable Customers
- Table Booking Analysis
- City Filter

---

### 3. Restaurant & Cuisine Analysis

This page analyzes restaurant and cuisine performance.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Restaurants: 56K
- Average Rating: 3.70
- Average Votes: 261
- Revenue per Restaurant: 2.93K

**Visuals:**
- Orders by Cuisine
- Revenue by Top 10 Restaurants
- Average Rating by Cuisine
- Restaurants by Rating
- Cost Bucket vs Orders

---

### 4. Delivery Performance

This page focuses on delivery efficiency and operational performance.

**Key Metrics:**
- Average Delivery Time: 44.55
- Late Orders: 96K
- Late Delivery %: 48.12%
- Cancelled Orders: 20K

**Visuals:**
- Average Delivery Time by City
- Delivery Status Breakdown
- Cancellation by City
- Delivery Performance by City
- Year-wise Analysis

---

### 5. Restaurant Detail

This page provides detailed analysis for an individual restaurant.

**Metrics & Analysis:**
- Average Delivery Time
- Total Revenue
- Average Votes
- Average Rating
- Total Orders
- Restaurant Name
- Revenue Trend
- Orders Trend
- Customer Type
- Delivery Status
